## ABOUT US

### How It All Started — "Our Beginnings"
Wings Melaka had its beginnings in **1997** when a group of Christian parents whose children had
special needs met together, hoping to start some services for children and adults with learning
differences. With the support of **Malaysian Care** and the community of churches in Melaka, this
project became a reality in **October 1998**, when Wings Melaka launched the **Early Intervention
Programme**. On **28 June 1999**, Wings Melaka became officially registered.

**Our Identity:** Wings Melaka is a not-for-profit organisation providing a range of programs
designed to help people, especially children, with learning differences and their families. While
the initial focus was on younger children, over the years it expanded to include school-age
children and young adults with learning differences. These programs maximize learning
opportunities through creative, empowering and transforming activities, helping them achieve their
maximum potential. Wings Melaka is open to people of all races and religions.

### Mission, Vision & Values
- **Our Mission:** Making a difference in the lives of people with special needs and their families
  by providing learning opportunities that will enable them to achieve their full potential.
- **Our Vision:** Reaching, Empowering, Transforming.
- **Our Values:** As an expression of God's love, Wings Melaka embraces the following values:
  **Compassion, Acceptance, Integrity and Hope.**

### Our Name — "The Story of Wings Melaka"
The story of Wings Melaka is a story of **HOPE**: hope in God, hope in our children, and hope in
ourselves as parents. In the face of all the challenges, we never lose hope — we look up and see
our children soaring on wings like eagles. Our name is taken from **Isaiah 40:31** in the Bible:
*"… but those who hope in the LORD will renew their strength. They will soar on wings like eagles;
they will run and not grow weary; they will walk and not be faint."*

## SERVICES

The "Our curriculum / 6 domains" list is shared by EIP and SAP:
**Reading, Writing & Early Number Skills · Gross Motor Skills · Language Development ·
Fine Motor Skills · Self Help Skills · Social Development.**

### 1. Early Intervention Programme (EIP)
Wings Melaka provides a friendly, safe and stimulating educational environment for **children aged
0 to 6** with learning differences [to] have the opportunity to maximise their potential and take
their rightful place in society. Wings Melaka recognizes the family as the fundamental unit of
society and acknowledges the importance of the family's role in the upbringing and well-being of
all children, including children with learning differences.

In line with research that children under 6 learn best from their parents/main carers, Wings Melaka
embraces a **Family-Centred Practice Model** requiring parents to accompany their child at the
Centre. Parents actively participate and are encouraged to take primary responsibility for their
child's early learning. Early childhood intervention teachers must have the knowledge, skills and
sensitivity to interact with both the young students and their parents/guardians/caregivers.

**What is involved:**
1. The child is first **assessed by our teachers**. The child may have learning differences such as
   autism, Down Syndrome, attention deficit disorder (ADD), attention deficit hyperactivity disorder
   (ADHD), Slow Speech, Global Developmental Delay, etc. Teachers work with the parents to assess
   the child's basic skills and behaviours.
2. An **Individualized Education Plan (IEP)** is drawn up for each child — every IEP is different,
   built from skills under each of the 6 curriculum categories.

### 2. School-Age Programme (SAP)
- Running since **June 2005**.
- Provides **school-readiness classes** to prepare children **aged 5 to 12** with learning
  differences for formal schooling, or to support those already attending regular school or special
  education programmes.
- Children will have gone through our **EIP**.
- Children are **not accompanied** by parents/caregivers.
- Curriculum: same **6 domains** as above.

### 3. Young Adults Programme (YAP) — ⚠️ *Temporarily Closed*
- Launched **January 2012**.
- For young people **aged 18 to 25** with recognised learning differences.
- Equips students with skills to attain gainful employment and enhance their quality of life.
- Ultimate goal: enable them to move on into **independent / supported living**.
- Curriculum (**8 areas**): Ongoing Education · Independent Living Skills · Recreation ·
  Community Skills · Social Skills · Art & Craft · Travel Training · Employment.

### 4. Toy & Resource Library
- Operational since **1998**.
- Provides toys on loan to **ALL** children with learning differences — whether in current
  programmes, graduated, or waiting for placement.
- Anyone who can show a relationship to people with learning differences can use the library.

### 5. Counselling / Family Therapist
- In place since **January 2008**.
- Provides opportunity for counselling or discussion with the counsellor.
- For the parents and other family members of Wings Melaka children.

### 6. Parent Support
**Parent Support Group** — met regularly ≥4 times a year since **1999**. Empowerment, awareness
and education of parents; opportunity for parent support and friendship; regularly invites speakers
(service providers, government officers, parents and staff).

**Coffee Session** — met regularly ≥2 times a year since **January 2007**. Informal gathering for
parents and by parents with staff supervision; deeper friendships between parents.
