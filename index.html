Who we are

A story of hope — since 1997

Wings Melaka began in 1997 when a group of parents whose children had special needs came together, hoping to build something better. Today we provide creative, empowering programmes for children and young adults with learning differences — open to people of all races and religions.

What we do

Programmes for every stage

From early childhood to young adulthood, each programme is built around the individual — with personalised plans and a family-centred approach.

🧸

Early Intervention Programme

A friendly, safe and stimulating environment where parents and children learn together, guided by individualised education plans.

Ages 0–6 · EIP

School-Age Programme

School-readiness classes preparing children for formal schooling, building on the foundations of early intervention.

Ages 5–12 · SAP
