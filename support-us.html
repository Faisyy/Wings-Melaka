  • Write a cheque made payable to Wings Melaka and post it to us.

Sponsor a Child

Through our Sponsor-A-Child programme, a sponsor donates a set amount each month to help students who are facing financial hardship — walking alongside one child's journey and helping to cover the cost of their learning and care.
